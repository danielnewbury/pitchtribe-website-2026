Features

Everything the cork board did, and much more

Four things every clubhouse needs, all in one open-source package. Run all of them on one screen or give each its own.

01 · Noticeboards

Dynamic digital noticeboards

Socials, AGMs, subs reminders, sponsor thank-yous and team shout-outs. Post them once and they rotate on every screen, with no more drawing pins or curling paper.

  • Drag-and-drop layouts with your club colours and crest
  • Expiry dates, so last month's quiz poster takes itself down
  • Scrolling ticker for quick news and results
  • Photos and short video clips from the weekend

02 · Fixtures

Scheduled events & fixtures

Show this weekend's fixtures, kick-off times, pitch allocations and results across every age group. Schedule the screens too, so the fixtures wall takes over on matchday morning.

  • Import fixtures from CSV or iCal feeds
  • Day-part playlists: matchday, midweek, function night
  • Home/away, kick-off, pitch and changing-room info
  • Results entered on the touchline, live in the bar

03 · Menus

Instant bar & kitchen menus

Changed the guest ale or run out of pies? Tap it on your phone and the menu board updates in seconds. Nobody has to find the laptop or reprint anything.

  • One-tap "sold out" and "back on" toggles
  • Prices, allergens and specials in one place
  • Happy-hour pricing that switches on automatically
  • Separate bar, kitchen and tea-hut boards

04 · Guest Wi-Fi

Built-in captive portal

Give visitors a branded Wi-Fi welcome page with your sponsors, fixtures and an optional mailing-list sign-up. A QR code on the TV gets them online without anyone asking for the password.

  • Branded splash page with terms and opt-in consent
  • On-screen QR join codes that rotate with the password
  • Time and bandwidth limits to protect the till and CCTV
  • Sign-ups export to CSV for your mailing tool

Made for volunteers

If you can send a text, you can run the screens

Most clubs are run by volunteers between shifts behind the bar. The Clubhouse dashboard works in any phone browser on the club network. There's no app to install and no training day.

  • Role-based logins for bar staff, coaches and the committee
  • Changes go live in seconds, with a preview first
  • Ready-made templates for every screen type
  • Keeps playing from local cache if the internet drops

How it works

From box to bar screen in an afternoon

No installers, no contracts. Flash a card, plug it into the TV, and manage everything from your phone.

Read the quick-start
  1. 01

    Flash

    Write the Clubhouse image to a microSD card with Raspberry Pi Imager. It already includes the player, the dashboard and the Wi-Fi portal.

  2. 02

    Plug in

    Connect the Pi 5 to the TV's HDMI port and to your network. It boots straight into full-screen signage with no keyboard needed.

  3. 03

    Claim

    Scan the pairing code on the TV with your phone, name the screen ("Bar left", "Tea hut") and choose what it shows.

  4. 04

    Run

    Add notices, fixtures and menus from the dashboard. Add more Pis and they all appear in the same place.

Hardware

Built on Raspberry Pi 5

The Pi 5 has enough power for smooth 4K slides, video and the captive portal on one board. It's small enough to tape behind the TV and uses very little electricity. If you need more screens, add another Pi.

Open source

Owned by your club, not a vendor

Clubhouse is free, open-source software. Your notices, fixtures and guest sign-ups stay on hardware your club owns. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you choose to send it.

No subscriptions

Pay once for the hardware and nothing else. There's no per-screen licence and no premium tier holding back the useful features.

Your data stays home

Runs on your local network. Guest Wi-Fi sign-ups are stored on your Pi and exported only when you want, which keeps GDPR simple.

Community-built

Templates, sport-specific fixture importers and translations come from clubs like yours. Fork it, theme it, contribute back.

FAQ

Questions from the committee

Do we need a technical person to set it up?

No. If someone at the club can plug in a TV and use a phone browser, they can set up Clubhouse. The image boots straight into signage and pairing is done by scanning a code.

Will the captive portal work with our existing router?

Yes. The Pi can act as the guest access point itself, or sit alongside your current router on a separate guest network so the till, CCTV and office PCs stay isolated from visitors.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Screens keep playing from local storage. The dashboard runs on your club network too, so you can still update menus and notices while the broadband is out.

Can we run more than one screen?

Yes. Each screen gets its own Pi 5, and they're all managed from the same dashboard. Group screens by room and push different playlists to each.

Can we show sponsor adverts?

Yes. Sponsor slides slot into any playlist, and Clubhouse logs how many times each one played. That's handy evidence when sponsorships come up for renewal.

Is it really free?

The software is free and open source. You only pay for the Raspberry Pi hardware and the TV you probably already have.
