The platform

One system for the whole matchday

From the big screen to the concourse, PitchTribe keeps every display on message, on time and connected to the live game.

Matchday Display OS

Control every screen from one place. Push line-ups, scores, safety messages and sponsor rotations to the right display at the right second.

  • Screen groups and zones
  • Scheduled and live content
  • Fail-safe playback

Fan Media Network

Turn passive screens into a two-way channel. Live stats, club storytelling and matchday experiences that fans actually look at.

  • Live scores and stats
  • Club and competition feeds
  • Mobile-connected experiences

Brand & Sponsor Delivery

Give partners measurable presence across the ground. Schedule, target and prove delivery with real-time reporting.

  • Campaign scheduling
  • Audience-aware placement
  • Delivery reporting
