What PitchTribe does

Everything your club needs on screen

Four powerful display modes, all managed from a single web dashboard accessible from any device on your club network.

Dynamic Digital Noticeboard

Publish announcements and news instantly from your phone. Updates appear on screen within seconds — no print runs, no laminating.

  • Unlimited notice categories
  • Scheduled publish & expiry times
  • Rich text, images and pinned items
  • Live scrolling ticker strip
Scheduled Fixtures & Events

Pull in match schedules and club events. The display rotates through upcoming fixtures and updates in real time when results come in.

  • Import from CSV or connect your league site
  • Matchday countdown timers
  • Multi-team and multi-sport support
  • Auto-archive past fixtures
Instant Bar & Kitchen Menus

Manage bar and food menus from a simple dashboard. Change prices, mark items sold out, or switch to a matchday special in seconds.

  • Real-time price and availability updates
  • Multiple menu boards per screen
  • Scheduled menus (lunch, evening, matchday)
  • Allergen information display
Guest Wi-Fi Captive Portal

Turn your Pi into a Wi-Fi gateway. Visitors connect and see a branded splash page before getting online — your branding, your message.

  • Custom splash page with club logo
  • Terms & acceptable-use acceptance
  • Bandwidth controls per session
  • No third-party portal service needed
Getting started

Set up in an afternoon

Three steps from unboxing to a live display. You need a Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB+), a microSD card, and an HDMI screen.

1
Flash the SD card

Download the PitchTribe OS image and flash it to microSD. One command installs Raspberry Pi OS Lite, the PitchTribe stack, and kiosk mode automatically.

pitchtribe-2.1.0-rpi5.img.xz
2
Connect and boot

Plug the Pi 5 into any HDMI display and power it on. PitchTribe boots straight into full-screen kiosk mode — no keyboard or mouse needed after first boot.

HDMI out → Power on → Done
3
Manage from anywhere

Open the PitchTribe dashboard on any phone, tablet, or laptop on your club Wi-Fi. Update notices, menus, and fixtures live — from the bar, the car park, wherever.

http://pitchtribe.local
