What PitchTribe does

Everything your club needs on screen

Four display modes, all managed from a single web dashboard on any device connected to your club network.

Dynamic digital noticeboard

Publish announcements and news from your phone. Updates appear on screen within seconds — no print runs, no laminating.

  • Unlimited notice categories
  • Scheduled publish & expiry times
  • Rich text, images and pinned items
  • Live scrolling ticker strip

Scheduled fixtures & events

Pull in match schedules and club events. The display rotates through upcoming fixtures and updates when results come in.

  • Import from CSV or your league site
  • Matchday countdown timers
  • Multi-team and multi-sport support
  • Auto-archive past fixtures

Instant bar & kitchen menus

Change prices, mark items sold out, or switch to a matchday special in seconds from a simple dashboard.

  • Real-time price and availability updates
  • Multiple menu boards per screen
  • Scheduled menus (lunch, evening, matchday)
  • Allergen information display

Guest Wi-Fi captive portal

Turn your Pi into a Wi-Fi gateway. Visitors see a branded splash page before getting online — your logo, your message.

  • Custom splash page with club logo
  • Terms & acceptable-use acceptance
  • Per-session bandwidth controls
  • No third-party portal service needed

Getting started

Set up in an afternoon

Three steps from unboxing to a live display. You need a Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB+), a microSD card and an HDMI screen.

  1. 1

    Flash the SD card

    Download the PitchTribe OS image and flash it with Raspberry Pi Imager. It bundles Raspberry Pi OS Lite, the PitchTribe stack and kiosk mode.

    pitchtribe-2.1.0-rpi5.img.xz
  2. 2

    Connect and boot

    Plug the Pi 5 into any HDMI display and power it on. PitchTribe boots straight into full-screen kiosk mode — no keyboard or mouse needed.

    HDMI out → Power on → Live
  3. 3

    Manage from anywhere

    Open the dashboard on any phone, tablet or laptop on your club network. Update notices, menus and fixtures live — from the bar, the car park, wherever.

    http://pitchtribe.local

Questions

Frequently asked

What hardware do I need? +

A Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB or 8 GB), the official 27 W USB-C power supply, a 16 GB+ microSD card, a micro-HDMI to HDMI cable and any TV or monitor. A case with a fan is recommended for always-on use.

Is it really free? +

Yes. PitchTribe is MIT-licensed open source. There are no subscriptions, per-screen licences or hidden fees — you only pay for the hardware.

Does it need an internet connection? +

No. Displays and the dashboard work entirely over your local network. Internet is only needed for optional features like league fixture imports and software updates.

Can I run more than one screen? +

Yes. Each Pi 5 drives up to two displays, and you can add as many Pis as you like — manage them all from one dashboard, each showing its own playlist.

Ready to get started?

Flash your Pi 5, plug it in and your first display is live in minutes. Free forever.

curl -sSL https://get.pitchtribe.media | sudo bash
